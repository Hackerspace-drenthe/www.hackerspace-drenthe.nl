MCH2022 is a nonprofit outdoor hacker camp taking place in Zeewolde, the Netherlands, July 22 to 26 2022. The event is organized [for and by volunteers](https://wiki.mch2022.org/Volunteering_Guide) from and around all facets of the worldwide hacker community.

Knowledge sharing, technological advancement, experimentation, connecting with your hacker peers and hacking are some of the core values of this event.

MCH2022 is the successor of a string of similar events happening every four years since 1989. These are [GHP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galactic_Hacker_Party), [HEU](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacking_at_the_End_of_the_Universe), [HIP](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacking_in_Progress), [HAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hackers_at_Large), [WTH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_the_Hack), [HAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacking_at_Random), [OHM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observe._Hack._Make.) and [SHA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Still_Hacking_Anyway).  
  
Similar events are 🇬🇧[Electromagnetic Field](https://www.emfcamp.org/),   🇩🇰[Bornhack](https://www.bornhack.dk/), 🇧🇪[Fri3d Camp](https://fri3d.be) and   🇩🇪[Chaos Communication Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_Communication_Camp).

Hackerspace Drenthe will be at MCH at the HSNL village (a joint village of the dutch hackerspaces). During this period the hackerspace in Coevorden will be closed because all staff it at MCH.
